Kyoto

Japan • Cultural Capital

Highly Photogenic Nature Safe for Solo

Kyoto, once the capital of Japan, is a city on the island of Honshu. It's famous for its numerous classical Buddhist temples, as well as gardens, imperial palaces, Shinto shrines and traditional wooden houses.

Real-Time Traveler Alerts

Transport Alert

Bus routes 100 and 206 (to Kiyomizu-dera) are currently experiencing high tourist volume. Consider subway + walking.

Tourist Scam Warning

Beware of "free tea ceremony" invitations near Gion from unofficial guides. Stick to highly-rated registered operators.

Hidden Gem

Otagi Nenbutsu-ji

Avoid the Arashiyama bamboo forest crowds. This quirky temple features 1,200 unique stone statues carved by ordinary citizens.

Arashiyama Area
Local Food

Nishiki Market Alleyways

Don't just walk the main street. The tiny perpendicular alleyways house the best, most authentic standing sushi bars.

Nakagyo Ward
Culture

Gion Etiquette

Do not photograph Geisha (Geiko/Maiko) on the streets without permission. Fines are actively enforced in private alleys.

Must Know
Secret Spot

Kamo River at Dusk

Grab convenience store snacks and sit by the Kamo riverbank in the evening. It's where locals go to relax and socialize.
